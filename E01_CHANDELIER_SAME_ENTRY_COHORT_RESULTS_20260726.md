## Standard path boundary

This job tests the registered Chandelier from entry. It does **not** use a
structural arm, adverse-WT divergence, partial clip, E02 runner or post-top
activation. Those are a later, separately preregistered adaptation in
`TOP_EXIT_RESEARCH_BACKLOG_20260726.md`. A result from this job must not be
credited to that future interaction, and that adaptation must not overwrite

## Fixed comparison contract

Every arm preserves the preselected entry timestamps, target/add multipliers,
next-RTH fills, $16,000 capacity and persistent lower/resting reclaim. LONG and
SHORT use separate cash/P&L ledgers. B&H uses $2,000. Every fill pays 5 bps
commission and 2 bps adverse slippage.

The rolling extreme becomes the stored reclaim/top reference after an exit.
Future HTF source count, entry-capacity breach and bars-flat-beyond-reclaim are
zero in every selected non-insolvent row.

## Responsiveness and interpretation

Every primary range is connected:

| knob | comparison groups with a changed fold fingerprint |
|---|---:|

| lookback 10/20/30/55 | 800 / 800 |

The standard path therefore has no inert/red knob range. Its weakness is
structural: from-entry Chandelier is a volatility-adjusted trailing stop, not a
top detector. Slow/wide settings approach leveraged hold and lose the E02
control; fast/narrow settings lower exposure or churn. That is precisely why
the backlog's structural-arm adaptation remains a separate next hypothesis.
