1 — Entries

Two macro buckets. Bottom buys an oversold dip (long) or sells an overbought top (short) — price stretched away from mean and turning. Breakout buys a Donchian/Δ/arrow break (long) or breakdown (short) — price confirming direction through structure. Every entry switch lives in one bucket; filters in §4 then gate the bucket they belong to. Short entries are mirrors: what is "bottom" for long is "top" for short.

2 — Reentries

Reentry = second+ entry into a position after the first close. PULL are dumb distance buckets (900+ combo illusion). B-blocks are smart conditional reentries (only 4 of 15 actually fire on stocks). Mandatory/Guaranteed forces reentry after a profitable close — the anti-churn emergency lane. Sweep reentries inside an entry bucket, never alone.

3 — Exits

Three exit families. Top/TP exits a long into strength (overbought / scorer fade) — mirrors a short's breakdown exit. Breakdown/Structure exits when price breaks the wrong way — the stop family. Trailing rides winners with an ATR/chandelier shelf. No-loss gates (§4) veto loss exits globally.

4 — Filters & Gates — who filters what

Filters are not strategies — they are vetoes. The table maps each gate to the entry/exit bucket it should gate. Testing a filter outside its bucket wastes the sweep. Example: RED_ZONE_TRADIER_GATE gates entries near option walls — almost entirely Bottom bounces near resistance; testing it on Breakout DC entries adds noise.
