# Switch-matrix interdependency and retest manual

The switch matrix is not an OFAT leaderboard. An entry, exit, reentry, sizing,
or master change alters the opportunity set seen by every downstream field.
Rows remain useful evidence, but they are valid only for their recorded
contract fingerprint and accepted per-symbol baseline.

## What the executable queue does and does not prove

The regenerated queue contains 894 executable, non-option Tradier config knobs.
It is a test plan, not evidence that all 894 currently work. Each row now
separates:

- its registry role from its safer executable role;
- reviewed activation dependencies from standalone/unresolved settings;
- vector-diagnostic versus exact-only backends;
- per-symbol, global-only, and nondeployable/no-live-read scope;
- default-control/type/runtime-fence grid defects; and
- the exact changed-fingerprint proof required before a result is useful.

Do not treat a shared three-token registry family as an activation dependency.
For example, `WT_DC_ENTRY_THRESHOLD` is not controlled by
`WT_DC_ENTRY_BAR_MATURITY_BLOCK_ENABLED`; only the bar-maturity value is.

## Required order

1. **Baseline:** same symbol, side, NPZ hash, completed-parent clock, capital,
   costs, and accepted per-symbol overlay.
2. **Masters and wiring:** prove each reviewed family master and one/two binding
   smoke values. A token-only source mention is not wiring proof. RED reconnect,
   gated-off, invalid-range, and degenerate fields do not enter the exact grid.
3. **Cross-cutting guards:** establish regime, alignment, risk and other guards
   before action-path comparisons; changing one conservatively invalidates the
   complete bundle.
4. **ENTRY paths:** screen coherent alternatives, including ladder, WT/DC,
   structural higher-high/higher-low, Golden Rule, and oscillator events.
5. **AUGMENT paths:** with the entry schedule frozen, test add timing and
   eligibility; every add changes later exposure and exit populations.
6. **REDUCE paths:** with entry+augment frozen, test partial reductions and
   accounting before full exits. A reduce is not an exit alias.
7. **Entry/action filters:** test only against their surviving upstream
   schedules. Filters are
   allowed to reduce bad entries; they cannot be judged on a dead entry path.
8. **EXIT and REENTER/RECLAIM together:** test top/structure exits with mandatory
   reclaim. A stop/bottom break is an emergency diagnostic, not the primary
   profit mechanism. Verify that a veto postpones reclaim and never forgets it.
9. **Sizing/ladder:** optimize multipliers only after schedule survival.
   Enforce side-isolated $16,000 capacity and compare against side-aware $2,000
   B&H. Capacity can reject adds and thereby change later actions, so a sizing
   change requires the coherent schedule replay unless exact fingerprints prove
   the schedule is unchanged.
10. **Exact survivors:** run ordinary stock V8 with resolved-config,
   schedule/accounting, holdout, solvency, fill, future-HTF, reentry, and data
   provenance checks.

## Coherent bundles

Screen ENTRY + AUGMENT + REDUCE + EXIT + REENTER/RECLAIM as one schedule.
Vary one family at a time, then test combinations within each group, followed
by a small cross-group beam/Pareto search and mandatory leave-one-out. Cache causal
indicators and schedules; batch symbols only in the vector/compiled screen.
Exact single-symbol confirmation remains isolated because shared capital and
ranking make a multi-symbol exact invocation a different experiment.

## Invalidation and retest triggers

Invalidate downstream results when any of these changes:

- engine, live decision code, account/side isolation, or accounting;
- per-symbol accepted overlay or a family master;
- entry schedule (invalidates filters, exits, reclaim, and sizing);
- exit/reentry schedule (invalidates sizing and exposure);
- reentry behavior (invalidates later exit evidence because reopened positions
  create a different exit population);
- sizing/capacity (invalidates the coherent schedule unless an exact
  schedule-fingerprint equality proof permits signal reuse);
- cross-cutting regime/alignment/risk guards (invalidate the complete bundle);
- NPZ hash, parent-clock provenance, interpolation policy, window, costs,
  capital, or B&H convention;
- resolved override value fails to match the requested typed JSON value.

A sizing-only change may reuse signal timestamps only after proving the complete
action schedule unchanged. It always requires accounting, capacity, solvency,
TIM, and B&H metrics to be recomputed. A reporting-description change
invalidates no numeric evidence.

Keep losing rows gray under their original fingerprint; do not delete them and
do not use them as current-contract proof.
