## QA CORRECTION NOTICE

The backtest framework had **5 critical bugs** found by `qa_controlfreak/`:

| Bug | Impact | Correction Factor |
|-----|--------|-------------------|
| Sharpe annualization: `sqrt(bars_per_year)` instead of `sqrt(trades_per_year)` | Inflated Sharpe 8-34x | Divide old Sharpe by ~10 for estimate |
| `2of3_aligned` stoch gate timestamp bug | Gate always open → inflated SHORT results | SHORT results pre-fix unreliable |
| `trailing_1atr` hasattr bug | Trailing stop never fired | Trailing stop results invalid |
| Stoch using close-only instead of H/L/C | Wrong stoch values | Minor impact on WR (~1-2%) |
| Missing commission (0.075%/side) | Inflated PnL by ~0.15% per trade | Subtract 0.15% from avg PnL per trade |

**Rule: Any Sharpe > 20 from pre-QA tests should be divided by ~10. Post-QA tests (RSI investigation, entry logic) are trustworthy.**

## PART 10b: REENTRY + RATIO FIXES (2026-03-26)

### BACKTEST_CHANGE_151 — Lower MIN_GAIN_TO_BUY_AGGRESSIVELY from 5.0% to 3.0%
**Files:** `config.py` (global + all 3 modes), `config_tradier.py`
**Problem:** 5% gate was too late — by the time a position has 5% gain, it's often near its TP exit. System can't build size on confirmed winners. Real data showed augments clustering at 3.0-3.1%.
**Analysis:** Full safety math at different gain levels × augment sizes × adverse moves:
- 3.0% gain + 50% augment → 2.8% buffer pre-reversal → survives 1.5% adverse 1h candle with 1.43% remaining ✅
- Tiered system: 0.4x=1.2% (pullback+stoch, 40% cap), 0.5x=1.5% (reduced 70%), 1.0x=3.0% (full 60%)
- All trading code uses `config.MIN_GAIN_TO_BUY_AGGRESSIVELY` — no hardcoded values
**Previous values:** global 5.0 (was 1.5), LIGHT 5.0 (was 1.4), BASE 5.0 (was 1.5), EXTREME 5.0 (was 1.4), Tradier 5.0 (was 1.15)
**Status:** ENABLED — 3.0% across all modes and Tradier

### BACKTEST_CHANGE_152 — Direction-favorable simple reentry
**File:** `ez_manage.py` (process_single_reentry_evaluation, before DC BREAKOUT fast-path)
**Problem:** After close, reentry required ALL of: stoch_ready (k_3m+k_15m), NOT invalidated by DC break, price above/below level, bounce K reset (k_3m pullback to 35/65), strong trend OR higher_high/lower_low. For a SHORT with price falling, if DC_HIGH_3M gets briefly broken → INVALIDATED → can't reenter even when price resumes falling.
**Fix:** New fast-path: if within **120 min** of exit AND direction favorable (k_3m + k_15m both aligned in our direction) AND WT confirms (wt1_15m vs wt2_15m) AND not exhausted (k_3m 15-85 range) → immediate REENTRY. Bypasses invalidation, bounce reset, DC structure checks.
**Status:** ENABLED (default on)

### BACKTEST_CHANGE_153 — Aggressive ratio rebalance dead zone
**File:** `ez_manage.py` (ratio_rebalance_loop)
**Problem:** 5pp dead zone meant ratio had to be >5 percentage points off target before ANY action. With 17 stuck losers in bear mode, this was too passive — system wouldn't open shorts to balance ratio.
**Fix:** Dynamic dead zone:
- CRASH/JUMP regime OR 5+ stuck losers (gain < -2%) OR extreme ratio (>3x) → **2pp** dead zone
- Normal conditions → **5pp** unchanged
- max_opens increased from `skew/5` to `skew/3` and cap raised to 10 when stuck losers >= 5
**Status:** ENABLED (default on)
